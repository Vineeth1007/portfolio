Lab note

A model is only as honest as the way it was tested.

Most of my work lives in domains where a confident wrong answer is expensive - flagging a fraudulent transaction, an intrusion on an edge device, a pathogenic variant, a finding on a clinical scan. So I optimise for evidence that survives scrutiny, not a number on a slide: train-only normalisation, identifier removal, stratified and temporal splits, ablations that prove the model leans on real signal.
