## Dark vs Light Theme Rules

Use dark mode when:

- The section is immersive, cinematic, technical, cyber, AI, or transition-heavy.
- Showing hero backgrounds, 3D scenes, cyber-city visuals, animated domain maps, role portals, and technical environments.
- The goal is mood, depth, or premium futuristic energy.

Use light mode when:

- The user needs to read, compare, verify, or contact.
- Showing detailed case-study evidence, skill lists, resume-like sections, metrics tables, contact information, or hiring-focused content.
- Trust and clarity matter more than spectacle.

Do not randomly alternate dark and light. Every theme shift must have a reason.

Suggested theme rhythm:

- Home hero: dark
- Proof metrics: dark glass / high contrast
- Role portals: mixed dark cards with bright highlights
- Data Scientist evidence sections: light
- Case-study detail pages: mostly light with dark headers
- ML Engineer systems sections: mostly dark
- Applied Scientist benchmark sections: dark scientific interface with light evidence panels
- Contact: clean light or high-contrast dark, but extremely readable
